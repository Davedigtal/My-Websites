03 / The person

Hello, I'm Kairo Vale.

Lagos-born producer and mix engineer building songs from tape hiss, thumb piano and rooms with good echo. Started making beats on a borrowed laptop at sixteen, and now makes records, film scores and live sets that sit somewhere between Afrobeats, soul and electronic.

Every track starts the same way: one honest melody, then everything else gets out of its way. Open to artists, filmmakers and brands who want a sound that feels handmade, not templated.
